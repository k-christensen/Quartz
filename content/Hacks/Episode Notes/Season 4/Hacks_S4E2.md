# What Stuck Out:
- Deb disposing of the carcass on her doorstep even though she has a staff who could take care of that for her
- Countering Ava's assertion in the previous episode that Deb is heartless, when Deb has the dream at the beginning of the coyote licking her face, she wakes up clearly spooked (even more so when she hears the coyotes howling). However, when she sees her dogs are scared, she tells them to come to bed with her
- When Ava comes to the office, she asks the receptionist which caption she should use for her instagram post about being on the Times cover. The options she gives are "Living La Vida Loca" or "Oops they let a dumb bitch on the times front cover". When the receptionist is clear she doesn't like either and she tells Ava to be sincere, she does it, but it's clear she wouldn't have if she hadn't gotten external validation to do that first.

# Themes:
## Self Deprecation as Survival Instinct
When Ava is coming up with her instagram caption for the photo of her and Deborah together, the choices she gives are "Living La Vida Loca" which is nonsensical and lighthearted but in a way that plays down her accomplishment, or "oops they let a dumb bitch on the times front cover," which is blatant self deprecation.
