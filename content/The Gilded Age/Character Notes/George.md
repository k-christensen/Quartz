# Season 1
## Episode 1
- Isn't keen to make new friends like Bertha is. 
    - Doesn't understand why Bertha is so insistent on breaking into society and making new friends
    - When Bertha is struggling, he tries to get her to foster her existing connections (asking why she can't just be with their old friends or invite her sister)
- Despite his confusion about Bertha's goals, he supports her
    - Especially evident when he visits the architect and tells him to come to Bertha's party
- Establishes ruthlessness quickly 
    - When he tells the one guy he'll build a railroad right next to his to put him out of business
    - When Clay immediately asks if he wants George to dig up dirt on the aldermen
