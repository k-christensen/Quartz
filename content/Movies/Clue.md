# Initial Thoughts
- Physical comedy very present, especially with Mr. Green constantly spilling his drink
- They do the thing a lot where they reuse the same joke, but it manages to still be funny every time they use it
- Everyone except for Plum dressed in the opposite color of their name (although this is most evident in the female characters)
- Mrs. Peacock wearing a peacock brooch
- The part that drives home the joke with the soup slurping is the way the other side of the table just looks at the people doing the slurping with confusion
- "Retire to the study where the mysterious host will reveal his intentions" and then it's Wadsworth doing the lore drop foreshadowing how it's him 
- The delivery of "you don't need any help from me sir"
- After White says that her husband's dick was cut off, all the men crossing their legs at the same time
- Fight between Mustard and Boddy was extremely three stooges
- Wadsworth knows about the evidence, Boddy knew he was going to fake death, but he didn't expect to actually die
- The whole reasons for blackmail scene has so many iconic lines
- The way the table Mr. Green sits on collapses right when Wadsworth says that everyone was unamerican, again big into the physical comedy with him, a guy that's meant to work for the CIA and be very clean cut white guy
- Wadsworth laying it on thick during his explanation about him inviting everyone
- A lot of them cramming into doorways and trying to get through tight spaces at the same time
- Peacock fainting and falling through Wadsworth's arms
- A lot of Mustard's comedy is based on him misunderstanding Wadsworth
- A lot of the comedy comes from each of the characters leaning into the tropes around their character archetypes
- The needle drops used are very 50s and good at reminding viewer when this is supposed to take place because Scarlet's hair isn't helping
- Mustard and Scarlet being the ones who find both secret passageways
- The group not even being surprised or alarmed when Yvette and cop are killed
- Wadsworth having the running joke of being long-winded and when he says long story short everyone says too late
- The soundtrack used for Wadsworth's explanation of who did it really adds to the comical nature of it
