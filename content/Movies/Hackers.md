# Initial Thoughts
- Character aesthetics pulling from queer nightlife because actual hackers were found to be too boring
- Trope establishing Dade as a child prodigy
- Club influence also evident in the movie title music
- So much gender non-conformity
- English teacher is extremely oaf that is aggressively straight coded with his wardrobe of plain brown T-shirt and tan khaki cargo shorts
- In the board room scene, the way the hacker is distinct is because of how aggressively queer coded they are and how alt fashion he is compared to everyone else in suits
- Plague is Scar levels of queer coded villain
- Plague is giving unrequited crush on Dade (Dade saying blow me and Plague saying thanks with a smile, I mean come on)
- Keith Harring style poster in Kate's room continuing the queer aesthetics
- Kate saying that if she wins, Dade gets to be her slave, bisexual domme energy here
- Again with the gender-nonconformity: Kate dreaming about Dade wearing a dress after that was part of the stakes and being into it
- Plague skateboarding everywhere, Dade rollerblading, and Kate on the motorcycle, the only people you see featured driving cars are establishment straight coded villains
- "Hackers of the world unite" so now the hackers are queer socialist coded
- The scene where the traffic lights are hacked also gives very anti-car
- The woman who's Plague's henchman wearing that sparkly suit, definitely not what her more straight counterparts would wear, but she's also not quite at hacker levels of queer non-conformity, but she's got a closer proximity
- So many of these graphics sequences went on way too long
- Razor and Blade are fully doing drag makeup, also the makeup Kate wears on her date is very alt with the graphic eyeliner
- Plague using his henchman who thinks they're gf/bf to take the fall
